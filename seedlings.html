Overview

From mother plant to planting day.

A weak seedling cannot be corrected later in the season. That is why we control the whole propagation chain ourselves — from registered mother stock through rooting, screening and cold-chain delivery — instead of buying in plants of unknown origin. Growers receive uniform plants with strong crowns, a documented variety and a phytosanitary certificate for every batch, so the season starts from a known baseline rather than a guess.

What we do

Inside the seedling programme

Registered mother stock

We propagate from registered mother plants of Korean cultivars, so every order starts from a known and stable genetic base.

Disease screening

Plants are inspected and screened for the main strawberry pathogens and viruses before they leave the nursery.

Controlled propagation

Runners are rooted under a documented climate and feeding programme, producing uniform plants with balanced roots.

Cold-chain delivery

Seedlings are packed and transported under temperature control so they arrive anywhere in Uzbekistan in planting condition.

Planting-window planning

Propagation is scheduled around your target harvest date, so the plants are ready in the week you actually need them.

Establishment follow-up

An agronomist checks establishment in the first weeks after planting and tunes irrigation and feeding with your team.
