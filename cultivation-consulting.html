Overview

Korean growing know-how, local water and climate.

A programme that works in Korea cannot be copied into Uzbekistan unchanged. Water chemistry, light, summer heat and winter heating costs are different, and the nutrient recipe has to be rebuilt around them. We start from your water analysis and your facility's real capabilities, then write a programme your team can actually run, and we train them to run it, in their own language.

What we do

The consulting programme

Crop & variety selection

Which crop and cultivar suit your facility, your market window and your team's experience.

Water & nutrition programme

A fertigation recipe built on your own water analysis, with EC and pH targets by growth stage.

Climate strategy

Temperature, humidity and ventilation settings by season, balanced against heating and cooling cost.

Grower training

Practical training for operators and agronomists, delivered on site and at our AKIS facility.

Season supervision

Regular visits and remote review of your data, with corrections made while they still matter.

Yield & quality review

An end-of-season analysis of yield, fruit quality and cost per kilogram, feeding into the next cycle.
