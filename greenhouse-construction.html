Overview

Engineering built around the crop and the climate.

A greenhouse is not a building with plants inside it. Structure, covering, climate control, irrigation and the growing system have to be designed as one, against a specific crop and a specific location — Central Asian summers, cold winters and limited water. Firmmit designs, supplies and installs that whole package. Where a facility already exists, reconstruction is usually faster and far cheaper than a new build: the shell stays, and the systems that actually determine yield are replaced.

What we do

The scope we take on

Site survey & feasibility

Soil, water quality, power, access and local climate data are assessed before a single line is drawn.

Structural design

Span, height, ventilation and glazing are chosen for the crop, the wind and snow loads and the budget.

Turnkey construction

Foundations, frame, covering and internal systems installed by our own construction team and local partners.

Reconstruction & retrofit

Existing greenhouses are re-covered, re-sealed and re-equipped with modern climate, irrigation and growing systems.

Climate & irrigation systems

Heating, ventilation, screening, fertigation and sensor networks supplied and integrated as one system.

Commissioning & handover

Systems are tested under load, settings documented and your operators trained before the facility is handed over.
