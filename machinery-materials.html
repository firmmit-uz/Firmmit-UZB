Overview

Specified properly, delivered on time.

Most greenhouse problems in the region are supply problems: the wrong emitter for the water, a substrate that behaves differently than expected, a controller nobody can service locally. We supply equipment we install and support ourselves, which keeps the specification honest. Firmmit combines its own manufactured smart-farm equipment with components from established Korean and international suppliers, and holds the parts that fail most often in stock.

What we supply

Structures & covering

Spare parts & service
