Overview

Grown, graded and delivered cold.

Strawberry is a fruit that punishes every gap in the chain. Firmmit controls that chain from its own greenhouses — harvest timing, grading, packing and temperature — so the fruit that reaches a shelf still looks and tastes the way it did at picking. Domestic supply runs through the season from our facilities in the Tashkent region. The first export shipments are scheduled for September 2026.

What we do

From row to buyer

Greenhouse-grown supply

Fruit from our own climate-controlled facilities, harvested to a plan rather than to whatever ripens.

Grading & packing

Sorted by size and quality and packed in punnets or trays to the buyer's specification.

Cold chain

Pre-cooling after harvest and temperature-controlled storage and transport all the way to delivery.

Domestic distribution

Regular supply to retail, HoReCa and wholesale buyers, with volumes agreed before the season.

Export sales

Export documentation, phytosanitary certification and freight arranged for regional buyers.

Contract growing

Agreed volumes and quality standards fixed ahead of the season, for buyers who need certainty.
